These tables describe earlier versions. The `phase2_final_*` scripted runs
and their manifests identify the results generated by the final evaluator.

These findings describe the scripted supervisor on the locked 20-scenario
screening split. The comparisons share the same fault episodes and plant metrics.
See the [gap analysis](../results/phase2_screen_e4/gap_analysis.json) for paired
differences and intervals, and the [failure counts](../results/phase2_screen_e4/failure_counts.csv)
for the full breakdown.

G1 underperformed the nominal workflow at 30% mismatch. Its full-vs-ablated
comparison also lost one of ten high-mismatch episodes. G2 never invoked bounded
DE on this screening run. A separate development regression demonstrates that
the tool can repair a rejected proposal, while the approved live-model experiment
remains open. G3 improved plant success relative to the symptoms-only comparator
by 45 percentage points. The full supervisor remained 35 points behind the
oracle-label baselines, and its 75% localisation rate was below the 85% target.

The additional scripted stress run succeeded on 8/18 episodes. Nine failures were
classified as tracking and one as no action. Because several held-out episodes
have two faults and the model searches single-fault hypotheses, results for that
split warrant separate treatment.

## Representative trajectories

Full JSON checkpoints are in `results/phase2_screen_e4/episodes/`. Use the
scenario ID plus `__phase2_agent__0.json` to open a trace.

| Scenario ID | Observed path and outcome |
|---|---|
| `p2-screen-z2-heater_degradation-mm00-s30` | The replay identified the degraded heater. The proposed derate passed ensemble validation. True final tracking errors were below 0.01 °C. |
| `p2-screen-z2-heat_loss_increase-mm00-s30` | Replay identified enclosure-wide heat loss. Both zones were derated to 37.4 °C and 34.7 °C. Validation passed and both tracked within 0.01 °C. |
| `p2-screen-z4-sensor_bias-mm00-s30` | Replay selected local insulation loss instead of sensor bias. The model approved a zone 0 derate to 35 °C. True tracking error in that zone remained 10.766 °C. |
| `p2-screen-z4-actuator_stuck_high-mm30-s30` | Replay located the stuck heater correctly. Some ensemble members predicted that other heaters could be isolated without shutting down. Shutdown validation and the fallback were rejected. No recovery action was applied and the true plant reached 66.62 °C. |

Seven of the nine full-supervisor failures involved tracking. Within this group,
some traces show wrong localisation and others show a sensor magnitude error
despite the correct label. More informative input excitation and explicit
sensor-step estimation could help distinguish those cases. Both safety failures
involved a stuck heater and rejected shutdown. The episode exposes a mismatch
between the fallback policy and the validation rule under uncertainty. A separate
hard interlock and an uncertainty-aware isolation policy require validation before
any use beyond the simulator.

## Revised screening after the robustness fixes

The tables above preserve the pre-fix measurements. The revised [screening run](../results/phase2_revised_e4/summary.csv) reran all 140 episodes. The full supervisor moved from 11/20 to 12/20 plant successes and from 2/20 to 0/20 post-action safety violations. It applied emergency power cuts in the two originally overheating cases, bringing their post-action peaks to 59.18 °C and 59.03 °C. One other screening episode now registers a shutdown that the simulator oracle deemed unnecessary. This is a production-availability tradeoff of the conservative emergency path.

The revised [stress run](../results/phase2_revised_stress/summary.csv) finished at 8/18 plant successes with zero post-action safety violations. Localisation on the screening split remained 15/20. The full supervisor remains below the nominal oracle-label baseline, which scored 18/20. These scripted runs establish simulator behavior and preserve the need for the live and confirmatory evaluations.
